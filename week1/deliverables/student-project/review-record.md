## Key Audit Takeaways

1. **Practitioner Realism:** Software teams reject tools that cause alert fatigue. Intermediate cheap actions (`request_tests`) bridge the gap between dangerous auto-approval and expensive human escalation.
2. **Probability Grounding:** Priors must reflect realistic defect base rates (~2–5%), not worst-case dread, while loss functions carry the burden of risk aversion.
3. **Preprint Integrity:** Empirical claims must be bounded by sample size; baselines must reflect actual production alternatives (linters and zero-shot LLMs).
